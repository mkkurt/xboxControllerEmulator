## Description

Ever wanted to play Xbox Cloud Gaming or GeForce NOW with your HOTAS joystick or racing wheel? CloudPad makes it possible.

**The Problem:**
Cloud gaming platforms only support Xbox controllers. If you have a Thrustmaster HOTAS, Logitech racing wheel,or any other controller, you're out of luck.

**The Solution:**
CloudPad is a universal controller emulator that transforms ANY HID-compatible controller into an Xbox controller in real-time. Auto-calibration takes 60 seconds. Zero latency. Works everywhere.

**How It Works:**
1. Connect your controller (USB)
2. Click "Auto-Calibrate" and press buttons when prompted
3. Install our Chrome extension
4. Start emulation
5. Play cloud games with ANY controller!

**Features:**
✅ Universal HID device support (HOTAS, wheels, joysticks, DIY controllers)
✅ Auto-calibration wizard (no manual configuration)
✅ Cross-platform (Windows, macOS, Linux)
✅ Browser extension for seamless integration
✅ Zero latency (<1ms overhead)
